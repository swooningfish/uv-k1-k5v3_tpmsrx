📡 How often sensors transmit
Sensor stateTypical
ParkedAsleep: very rarely or not at all. Ford sends once after stopping, then about every 6 hours.
Driving (over about 20–25 km/h)About once a minute, a short burst of a few repeated frames (Ford about 4).
Fast pressure changeStraight away, then every few seconds while it keeps changing.
TPMS trigger toolStraight away; a burst of frames (Ford sends 12 in learn mode).

Figures vary by maker. On a parked car a step with nothing waking the sensor records nothing, so trigger it every few seconds with a tool, or let a little air out in short bursts. Make each step longer than the gap between your triggers, so every step gets several bursts. Driving gives one burst a minute: too slow to sweep, fine to check a setting you already found.

Steps

  1. Plug the K-plug cable into the radio and this PC.
  2. Close UV Studio and K5Viewer: they share the cable.
  3. Start TPMS CAP on the radio. It should show K-PLUG 115200: KPLUG.HTML.
  4. Press Connect and pick the cable's port.
  5. Pick the demod: FSK for Ford and Toyota, OOK for Schrader, GM, BMW / Mini and Nissan / Subaru (key 4 on the radio).
  6. Wake the sensor next to the radio. Use UP/DOWN, * and 3 on the radio to try offsets, IF presets and SENS levels.
  7. Press Save capture and Save log to send them back for debugging.
